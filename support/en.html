Frequently asked questions

What do I need for automatic rakah tracking?

Automatic tracking of rakahs, sujood and sahw requires an Apple Watch (watchOS 10 or later). The iPhone alone cannot measure the movement of your wrist; without a watch you can still use prayer times, the qibla, manual "Mark prayed" logging, qada, statistics and the other features. Detection is an aid and can make mistakes; you can always correct your records manually.

How do I calibrate?

On the watch, open Calibration and start the spoken calibration. Wear the watch on your usual wrist so that it does not slide. Follow the spoken guidance from takbir to salam: takbir, qiyam, ruku, standing up straight, sajdah, sitting between the two sajdahs, second sajdah, final sitting, salam to the right and to the left. After each instruction you are given time to move into the posture. Keep the same position and strap fit during calibration and prayer. After a successful calibration, continuous detection is switched on automatically.

Calibrate again if the watch position or strap fit has changed, or if detection is often confused.

What is the "second position"?

If your watch slides down your wrist (towards the hand) during prayer, you can teach it that position too. First calibrate in the normal position, then choose Calibrate second position, slide the watch 1–2 cm down and repeat the same steps. Detection uses whichever position fits better. If your strap fit changes, renew the second position as well.

Detection counted wrongly or missed my prayer. What should I do?
  • Mark the prayer manually with Mark fard prayed on the watch or Mark prayed on the iPhone.
  • If detection missed a prayer you prayed on time, correct it on the iPhone in your history with Mark prayed on time.
  • If a fard prayer does not pass the sequence check, the watch asks "Did you pray the … fard?"; answer "Yes, I prayed" or "No".
  • Make sure the watch fits well on your wrist; if it slides, calibrate the second position or renew the calibration.
  • If the problem continues, send a diagnostic recording for that prayer (see below).
How do I send a diagnostic recording?
  1. On the watch, go to Detection settings → Diagnostic recording, tap Start recording, pray, then stop with End recording.
  2. Tap Send recordings to iPhone (Salah Sense must be open on the iPhone).
  3. On the iPhone, open Settings → Diagnostic recordings, select the recording and send it with Share recording by e-mail to salahsensetracking@gmail.com.

The recording contains only motion and pressure values and the detection state; it contains no location, audio or account information. See the privacy policy for details.

How do excused days work?

During excused days (menstruation or postnatal bleeding) prayer and fasting are not performed. In Excused days, start the period and end it when it is over. During this period no prayer notifications are sent, no qada or points are recorded, detection and "Mark prayed" are turned off, and tahajjud and duha tracking pause. You can edit or delete past periods. This information is stored only on your device.

Prayer times differ from my local timetable. Why?

Choose the calculation method suitable for your country from the 12 available (on the watch under Calculation method, on the iPhone in Settings) and your madhab, and check that your location or city is correct. If the Hijri date is off by one day, use the Hijri date adjustment setting.

Does continuous detection affect battery and Activity rings?

Yes, it can. To keep detection running while the screen is off, the watch uses a workout session. No workout is saved to the Health app, but battery use may increase. If you prefer, you can turn continuous detection off in Detection settings.

How do I delete my data?

All data is on your devices; we have no server. Deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch deletes all data. You can also delete your calibration, learned heights and diagnostic recordings individually in the app.
