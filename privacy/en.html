Privacy Policy

Salah Sense · Effective date: 15 October 2026

In short: Salah Sense has no server. The app has no account, sign-in, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools. Your data is processed and stored only on your devices; it is not sent to us and we cannot access it.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Salah Sense iPhone and Apple Watch apps, their widgets, Live Activity and complications, as well as the Android phone and Wear OS versions.

2. Data processed on your device

Location. If you grant location permission or enter a city/coordinates, your location is used only to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction on the device. Approximate (kilometre-level) accuracy is requested. The device compass heading is read for the qibla indicator. Your location is never sent to any server. Without permission, you can enter a city and coordinates manually.

Motion and barometer (watch). For automatic rakah and sajdah tracking, calibration and voice practice, the watch's motion sensors (acceleration, rotation, direction of gravity) and, where available, its barometer (air pressure and the resulting relative height of your wrist) are processed on the device. Raw sensor data is not stored continuously. Your calibration profile, posture heights learned from your prayers, prayer results and a limited number of short movement summaries for each detected fard prayer are stored on the device.

Apple Health (Apple Watch only). If you allow it, the app reads your heart rate; it is used only on the device to help tell whether you are resting or moving, and it is not stored. So that movement detection can continue while the screen is off, a workout session is started on watchOS; this is why Health workout permission is requested. The app does not save workouts, heart rate or any other data to the Health app. Health data is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, is not shared with anyone and is not written to iCloud.

Prayer records and settings. Your prayers, qada list, duha and tahajjud records, fasting records, excused days, dhikr counts, points, statistics, language and settings are stored on your device.

Notifications and alarms. Prayer notifications, suhoor and iftar reminders and alarms are scheduled on your device; no remote (push) notifications are used.

3. Data that leaves the device (only at your request)

Sync between watch and phone. If you turn it on (it is off by default), prayer history, excused days and fasting records are transferred between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity link. Diagnostic recordings made on the watch are also sent to the iPhone over this link. On Android, transfer between the phone and the Wear OS watch uses the Wearable Data Layer of Google Play services. None of these transfers go to a server of ours.

Diagnostic recordings. To investigate detection problems, a diagnostic recording is created only if you start it. It contains motion sensor values, air pressure/relative height, detection state, calibration information and the app version; it contains no location, audio, name, e-mail or account information. It leaves the device only if you choose a destination in the system share menu (for example e-mail). If you e-mail it to us, we use it only to improve detection and to reply to you, and we delete it on request.

Adhan sound address (Apple Watch). If you enter an internet address for the adhan sound, the sound file is downloaded directly from that address. This request passes standard connection information such as your IP address to the owner of that server, which is not ours.

Source link. If you tap the link to the Namaz İlmihali of the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye (Diyanet) in the prayer information section, the PDF opens in your browser and that website's own policy applies. The app never opens this link by itself.

Backups. On iPhone and Apple Watch, app data may be included in Apple's standard device backup (iCloud or computer) depending on your device settings; diagnostic recordings are excluded from backups. The Android version turns off cloud backup and device transfer; you can export a backup file only to a location you choose.

4. Third-party components

Prayer times are calculated on the device with the open-source Adhan library, which makes no network connections. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social media SDKs. The Android version uses Google Play services for phone–watch transfer; Google's own privacy policy applies.

5. No tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not sell data.

6. Deleting your data

Because all data is on your devices, deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch (or your Android phone and watch) also deletes the data. Within the app you can also delete your calibration, learned heights, diagnostic recordings and individual records. You can ask us by e-mail to delete any e-mail or diagnostic recording you sent us.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone; we do not knowingly receive data from children under 13. If you believe a child has e-mailed us, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

The only data we hold is e-mail you send us yourself, with any attachments. You can request access to, correction or deletion of it by e-mail. Your rights under the data protection law of your country (for example the GDPR) are unaffected.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the current text will be published on this page with a new effective date. If the way data is processed changes significantly, we will also say so in the app update notes.
