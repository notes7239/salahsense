Salah Sense Help

Using the app and its settings · phone and watch

Important: Automatic rakah and sajdah tracking requires a watch (Apple WatchWear OS watch); a phone alone cannot measure wrist movement. Detection is an aid and can make mistakes: watch position, strap fit and personal movement differences affect it. You can always correct records by hand. The app does not issue religious rulings or judge whether a prayer is valid.

For FAQs and contact details see the support page. Bold words are button and menu names in the app.

1. Getting started

  • The iPhoneAndroid phone app shows prayer times, qibla, dhikr, history and settings. The Apple WatchWear OS watch app follows your movements during prayer and handles adhan alerts and calibration.
  • First open the Settings tabthe Location and settings page on the phone and set location, calculation method, Asr calculation and Gender (sections 2 and 8).
  • Then calibrate once on the watch; automatic tracking is only reliable after that.
  • Without a watch you can still use prayer times, qibla, dhikr, manual Mark prayed records, make-up prayers, statistics, the Hijri calendar, the imsakiyah and widgets.
  • Prayer Guide has offline summaries, with sources, on the daily prayers and sajdah sahw. The Prayer Supplications page gives the supplications recited in the prayer with Arabic text, transliteration and meaning. On the phone's Dhikr screen, Tasbih mode keeps the three counts of 33 after the prayer in order.
  • Daily Supplications in the Dhikr tab lists the Introduction and the fourteen wirds of Ibn ʿArabī’s prayer book al-Awrād al-usbūʿiyya (The Weekly Wirds): one for every night and every day of the week. The Now card shows the wird to recite: from the evening prayer time until morning, the night wird of the following day; from morning until evening, that day’s wird. A supplication opens as one whole text; with the Arabic, Transliteration and Meaning buttons at the top you choose which parts are shown. Quranic quotations within the prayers are not translated; their meaning is given in the footnotes at the end. You can remove wirds from the list, add them back with Add supplication, or add your own supplications with a title, Arabic, transliteration and meaning; your own supplications are stored only on this phone. With the Aa button at the top right you choose the transliteration system (DIN 31635 or İSAM), the Arabic text size and the page tone; these are shared with Memorize. Divine names, Quran verses and salawat are shown in separate colors. In the meaning text, divine names are not translated; they are written as their Arabic name in transliteration. Tap a verse or its number to see the surah and verse number, the Mushaf text and the translation; tap a divine name to see its meaning. You can change or turn off the colors in the Colors section of the Aa button.
  • In the Dhikr tab, below the Tasbih and Free count cards, you can keep your own dhikr. With New dhikr, enter the transliteration and, optionally, the Arabic text and meaning; choose a count of 33, 99, 100, a custom target or free counting, and pick a color. Each time the target is reached, the device vibrates strongly. Dhikr with Also show on watch turned on can be chosen on the watch's Dhikr screen; each device keeps its own counters. The list also comes with built-in dhikr: four supplications and 33 Quran verses. Built-in dhikr appear only on the phone; you can turn on Also show on watch for any of them. You can edit or delete the built-in dhikr.
  • To share history, turn on Share prayer history with my paired iPhone/Apple Watch in Settings → Paired device on the iPhone and Sync history in Device pairing on the watch. It is off by default; Sync now also sends older records.
  • Phone–watch sync needs the app on both devices; without a connection, records stay on the device. If needed, use Location and settings → Retry sync.
  • On first launch the app opens in the device language, or in English if that language is not supported. Change the language in Settings → Language on the phone and Language on the watch; each device keeps its own choice.
  • Times follow the device's 12/24-hour setting. In every language, dates use the Gregorian calendar and the digits 1, 2, 3; the Hijri date is shown separately with your Hijri date adjustment (section 9).
  • Settings → Appearance: Automatic follows the phone's light/dark setting; if you choose Light or Dark, the app and help follow it, as does the Automatic page tone of Memorization. The watch is always dark; home screen widgets follow the phone's own setting. The choice is stored on this device.

2. Location and prayer times

  • iPhone: Settings → Location and time zone. Tap Use my location and allow location, or fill in City, Latitude, Longitude and IANA time zone (e.g. Europe/London), then tap Save city/location.
  • Location and settings: tap Get my location, or fill in Location name, Latitude, Longitude and Time zone, then tap Save.
  • Location permission is optional; you can enter the city and coordinates by hand. Update location and time zone when you travel.
  • Calculation method (12 methods): Türkiye (Diyanet), Muslim World League, Egyptian General Authority, Karachi (Univ. of Islamic Sciences), Umm al-Qura (Makkah), Dubai, Moonsighting Committee, North America (ISNA), Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Tehran. Choose the one closest to your local timetable.
  • Asr calculation: Standard uses a shadow of one object length (first Asr), Hanafi two lengths (second Asr). The Turkish timetable uses standard.
  • Madhab: Sunni (including sunnah and witr) or Shafi'i (fard). It only sets which rakahs are tracked, not the times. On the watch: Madhab / rakahs.
  • Times refresh after a change; tap Refresh prayer times if needed. On the watch, method and Asr are under Calculation method.
  • Times are calculated on the device. The app is not an official timetable and may differ from yours by a few minutes.

3. Notifications and alarms

Prayer time notification

  • Notify when prayer time begins sends a notification for each prayer time. Set the timing with the stepper: At prayer time, or up to 60 minutes before (… min before) or after (… min after). The watch has the same setting in Adhan alerts.
  • When the watch handles these notifications, the iPhone does not schedule its own; they reach the watch while the iPhone is locked.
  • Location and settings: turn on Reminder and choose At prayer time or … min before. If notifications are late, grant Scheduling permission.

Alarm before the time ends

  • Alarm before the time ends: Off or a number of minutes before the prayer time ends. If the prayer is not recorded as prayed, it rings even in silent mode.
  • Prayers with an alarm: with the alarm on, each prayer has its own switch; it rings only for the selected prayers (for example only Fajr). Default: all on.
  • It may still ring if a record detected by the watch has not reached the phone yet.
  • Test the alarm: sets the same alarm for 1 minute later; lock the phone and wait. You can see whether the alarm rings on the phone without waiting for the morning. The test alarm rings for at most 1 minute.
  • A real alarm needs iOS 26; otherwise, or without alarm permission, a notification is sent instead.
  • 45 and 30 minutes before the end, the watch plays the hayya part of the adhan with vibration (Come to prayer, come to success), and once more strongly if you are asleep. If you seem to be sitting or lying idle while there is time, you get one You seem available notification (never in a prohibited time).

Tahajjud and Fajr alerts (watch)

  • In Settings → Adhan alertsAdhan alerts on the watch, Tahajjud alert follows the last third of the night and Fajr adhan alert follows imsak.
  • Alert time: At prayer time or 5–60 minutes before. Snooze: 1–15 minutes.
  • Moving silences the alarm briefly; tapping Mark prayed stops it. Silencing movement sets how much movement is needed: Less movement (sensitive), Medium, More movement (hard).
  • The nearest alarm plays your adhan sound, other alerts use the notification sound. Opening the watch app renews the nearest alarm.
  • Sound and vibration come from the device notification channel (Alert sound and vibration); the app has no built-in adhan recording.

Adhan sound (Apple Watch)

  • Adhan sound (or Adhan alerts → Choose adhan sound): enter the https address of a CAF, AIFF or WAV file and tap Download. Up to 30 seconds plays in notifications; longer files play only in the app.
  • Select an installed sound; use Play, Stop and Delete. With none selected, the built-in alarm sound plays.
  • Hayya segment (repeat + vibration): mark the "Hayya ala's-salah, Hayya ala'l-falah" part with Start and End seconds, set Repeat, and test with Play segment.
  • The file is downloaded directly from that address; the server is not ours.

Notification permission, silent mode, Focus, battery saving and volume can all affect alerts.

4. Calibration on the watch

Calibration teaches the watch your own takbir, qiyam, ruku, sajdah and sitting postures. It is done on the watch only.

  1. Wear the watch snugly on your usual wrist (right or left).
  2. Calibration → Personal calibration wizard → Start spoken calibrationCalibration → Start spoken calibration.
  3. Follow the voice: takbir (hands raised), qiyam, ruku, standing up, sajdah, sitting between sajdahs, second sajdah, final sitting, salam right, salam left. After each instruction you have time to take the posture, then a short measurement follows; hold still comfortably.
  4. With Learn the end-of-supplication gesture (optional) on, wiping the hands over the face after salam is also measured; or tap Skip the supplication gesture.
  5. Stop pauses; Resume this step continues.
  6. At the end you see Calibration complete and your wrist heights relative to qiyam. Tap Done, return to prayer.
  • Salam is voice-guided only; head movement is not measured.
  • After a successful calibration, continuous detection turns on automatically.
  • If postures were too similar or the takbir was not recognized, restart on the same wrist and hold your hands raised in the takbir step.

Second position

  • If the watch slides down your wrist during prayer, teach that position too: after the normal calibration tap Calibrate second position, slide the watch 1–2 cm towards the hand and repeat the steps (including wiping the face).
  • Detection uses whichever position fits better. A new normal calibration keeps the second position; if the strap fit changed, use Recalibrate second position or Delete second position.

When to recalibrate

  • When the watch position, strap fit or wrist changes, or detection is often wrong. A Check the watch position notification also means the watch is loose.
  • The app also learns wrist heights from your prayers. After a big change use Reset learned heights; Delete calibration removes the whole calibration.
  • Voice practice lets you practice with voice guidance; it creates no record.

5. Automatic tracking during prayer

  • Open the watch app within the prayer time and start with the opening takbir. Each part (sunnah, fard, witr) starts with its own takbir. When raised hands are seen, Takbir posture detected; move into qiyam. appears.
  • The order follows the opening takbirs: Fajr 2+2 · Dhuhr 4+4+2 · Asr 4+4 · Maghrib 3+2 · Isha 4+4+2+3 witr. Maghrib starts with the fard. With Shafi'i (fard), only the fard is tracked.
  • The screen shows the current part, the next takbir and the rakahs detected.
  • To start directly with the fard, tap Skip the first sunnahStart with fard.
  • Sajdah sahw is made at the end of the prayer, in the final sitting; if detected, the record shows Prostration of forgetfulness performed.
  • When a prayer section starts with its opening takbir, the watch turns on Water Lock so the screen is not touched by accident in sajdah. Turn the Digital Crown to unlock. If you unlock it while the section is still open, the watch asks Water Lock is off: I will repeat this section starts the count over with a new opening takbir; Continue keeps the section going. If you don't want it, turn it off in Detection settings → Water Lock; detection is not affected.
  • I will repeat this section restarts the current part. If saving failed, tap Retry saving.
  • To pray a section again from the start, tap I will repeat this section on the watch.
  • If calibration is missing, a Personal calibration wizard button appears.

Wrong count or missed prayer

  • If too many or too few rakahs are detected, the watch warns you (Check the rakah count). Check your count.
  • If you notice a mistake and want to pray the section again from the start, hold your hands at takbir height for about 5 seconds, then lower them into qiyam: the watch vibrates, shows Long takbir: counting this section again from the start. and the count starts over. You do not need to touch the screen. An ordinary takbir, qunut and raising the hands for more than 10 seconds do not change the section; after a fully prayed section, a long takbir opens the next section.
  • If you ended a section with too few rakahs with the dua and pray it again but its opening takbir is not recognized, it is counted as the repeat once its first rakah is complete.
  • If a count or sajdah warning appears for a section during a make-up session, it is shown on the watch: I will pray it again waits for the same make-up again from a new opening takbir; Continue as is counts the section as prayed, keeps the warning in the record and moves on to the next make-up.
  • If a section's opening takbir is detected during a prohibited prayer time (45 min after sunrise, 10 min before Dhuhr, 45 min before Maghrib), the watch vibrates and shows Prayers are not performed during a prohibited prayer time.; the record does not change. In the Hanafi practice (sunnah + witr included), no warning is given for that day's Asr fard in the prohibited time before Maghrib.
  • Mark the prayer by hand: Mark fard prayedMark prayed on the watch, Mark prayed on the phone. On the phone, Mark prayed asks first: Mark as prayed saves it, Cancel saves nothing. A fard marked by hand shows in the watch’s prayer sequence with who marked it and when (for example “Prayed” on phone · 21:09); a mark from another device is orange.
  • If you prayed on time but detection missed it, after the time ends open History on the phone, tap Mark prayed on time, set Time you prayed and tap Save as prayed. The time must be within the prayer time; this cannot be undone.
  • You can undo a wrong record in history with Undo record and then tap Mark prayed again.

Continuous detection and battery

  • Detection settingsPrayer detection → Continuous detection (experimental) keeps detection running with the screen off or on the watch face.
  • For this the watch keeps a workout session open all day. No workout is saved to Health, but battery use rises and Activity rings may be affected. If the session stops, reopen the app.
  • Background detection drains the battery faster. If the app is force-stopped, reopen it.
  • When it is off, detection works only while the app is open.

Optional prayers

  • Tahajjud and Duha times appear on the prayer times screen; mark them within their time with Mark prayed · +500. The watch can also detect them from the opening takbir. Both are optional, so the obligatory prayer comes first: Tahajjud is detected only after Isha's fard, final sunnah and witr are recorded, Duha only after that day's Fajr fard is recorded. With the Shafi'i madhab setting, Tahajjud doesn't wait for witr.

6. Sequence check and confirmation

Before a detected fard is saved, the sequence is checked: opening takbir, a ruku and two sajdahs in each rakah, the final sitting, sajdahs in the same direction, and no walking-like turn.

  • If the check fails, the watch vibrates and asks Did you pray the … fard? with the reasons. The time list shows Awaiting confirmation.
  • Yes, I prayed: saved with the detected finish time as Automatic detection (confirmed).
  • No: nothing is saved; reminders for that prayer time return.
  • No answer by the end of the prayer time: saved as Automatic detection (not confirmed, please check), so a performed prayer is not lost. Tapping Mark prayed meanwhile removes the question.

Marked by: … labels

  • Automatic detection: the fard passed the check.
  • Automatic detection (confirmed) / Automatic detection (not confirmed, please check): see above.
  • Automatic detection (fallback record, please check): detection counted the fard's rakahs exactly, but the normal save step failed, so a backup path wrote it.
  • “Prayed” on watch, “Prayed” in watch notification, “Prayed” on phone, “Prayed” in phone notification, “Prayed on time” correction, Mark made up: manual marks.
  • Labels asking you to check are orange in history; review and correct if needed.

7. History, make-up prayers and score

  • The History tabThe History page on the phone shows each prayer as Prayed, Made up or Pending / missed, with Prostration of forgetfulness performed and the marked-by label where relevant.
  • Prayer times that end unrecorded go to the make-up list (Make-up prayers due: …). When you make one up, tap Mark prayed next to that prayer; on the watch use Make-up prayers; after a time ends its button becomes Mark made up.
  • On the watch, Make-up prayers › Make-up tracking: Off (default), After the daily prayer: this prayer's make-up (1–10 make-ups of that prayer's fard; after Isha each one is the Isha fard followed by witr, and the witr is only counted), After the daily prayer: day make-up (a 1- or 2-day make-up set: Fajr → Dhuhr → Asr → Maghrib → Isha → Witr) or Make-up sets independent of prayer times. Day make-ups and sets need the make-up ledger. When the prayer's sequence is complete, the chosen make-up starts by itself; you are never asked at the start of the prayer time. It does not start during a prohibited time or in an excused period. On the main screen, the make-ups to pray after the daily prayer are listed one by one below the prayer's sequence and checked off as you pray them. End make-up detection stops it for this prayer time; Restart waits for a new opening takbir for the section being prayed, or starts the remaining make-ups again. With independent sets, the make-up set is always on the main screen: 1-day set / 2-day set starts one whenever you like, and Continue set resumes it from its step.
  • Travel mode: turn it on or off in Settings on the phone or the watch; phone and watch stay in sync. While it is on, the main screen shows Traveler and the fard of Dhuhr, Asr and Isha is expected as 2 rakahs; the Fajr and Maghrib fard, the sunnahs and witr do not change. A Dhuhr, Asr or Isha missed while traveling is counted separately in the make-up list ("from travel") and is made up as 2 rakahs, even at home.
  • Score: praying earlier in the time scores higher; a missed prayer lowers the score. Optional prayers add a bonus (Mark prayed · +500).
  • Note: The scoring system is included only to encourage believers to perform the daily prayers on time. May Allah accept your prayers. Ameen!
  • Statistics: weekly, monthly and yearly summaries and punctuality.
  • Create backup file saves your fard history to a file you choose; Restore backup merges it back.
  • A missing record does not prove a prayer was missed; the app only keeps marks made by you and by detection.

8. Excused days

  • This section appears only when SettingsLocation and settings → Gender is Female. On the iPhone it is under Calculation and reminders; the watch menu has a Gender row. With Select or Male it is hidden; changing the choice does not delete saved periods.
  • Settings → Excused days (also in the watch menu)Excused days: tap Start period when a period begins and End period when it ends. A warning appears if a period stays open long.
  • Edit past periods with Edit (Start, End, Ongoing) and Save, or remove them with Delete period.
  • During a period there are no prayer time, suhoor or iftar notifications, no make-up debt or score; detection and Mark prayed are off and Tahajjud/Duha tracking pauses. Times and widgets show Excused day.
  • Only you enter periods. They are stored on your device and, with sync on, shared with your other device along with history.

9. Hijri calendar and Ramadan

  • The Hijri date on the times screen follows the Umm al-Qura calendar. If it is a day off from your local calendar, choose −1, 0 or +1 in Settings → Hijri calendarLocation and settings → Hijri date adjustment.
  • Religious days lists upcoming religious days; the next one also appears on the times screen.
  • Ramadan timetable shows imsak and iftar times for Ramadan.
  • Suhoor reminder: Off or … min before imsak (30, 45, 60, 90). Iftar notification notifies at iftar. Both are scheduled only in Ramadan.
  • Fasting: in Ramadan the times screen shows the countdown to imsak or iftar and an I fasted button; a marked day shows Fasted.

10. Widgets, Live Activity and complications

  • The Next prayer widget counts down to the next prayer time or the end of the current one. Add it to the Home Screen (small, medium) or Lock Screen: touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Salah Sense.
  • Live Activity (in Settings) shows a countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; it does not start during an excused period.
  • Apple Watch complications: edit the watch face and choose Salah Sense (rectangular, inline, circular, corner).
  • The Android Home Screen widget shows the next prayer time; touch and hold the Home Screen and add Salah Sense from the widgets.
  • Wear OS offers a Salah Sense tile and complication.

During an excused period, widgets and complications show Excused day.

11. Sending a diagnostic recording

If detection keeps getting a prayer wrong, a diagnostic recording helps us investigate.

  1. On the watch: Detection settings → Diagnostic recording → Start recording. Wait 3 seconds with your hands down, pray all parts, then tap End recording. A recording lasts at most 20 minutes.
  2. On the watch, turn on Diagnostic recording on the Prayer detection page while detection runs, then pray.
  3. Send recordings to iPhone (with the app open on the iPhone); if needed Resend latest recordingSend recordings to phone.
  4. On the phone: Settings → Diagnostic recordings → choose the recording → Share recordingPrayer detection → Share, and email it to salahsensetracking@gmail.com.
  • Recording with the screen off needs continuous detection.
  • For each fard saved by detection, a short Automatic fard summary of the last minutes is kept and can be shared the same way.
  • Recordings contain motion sensor values, pressure/relative altitude, detection state, calibration data and app version; no location, audio, name or account data. They leave the device only if you share them. Recent recordings are kept; delete with Delete recording.

12. Troubleshooting and support

  • Times differ: check calculation method, Asr calculation, location and time zone, then tap Refresh prayer times.
  • No notifications: check notification permission, silent mode and Focus in the device settings. Battery saving and Scheduling permission matter too.
  • Detection does not start: on No motion sensor data received. reopen the watch app; on Takbir measurement is missing. Start spoken calibration. calibrate; for screen-off use turn on continuous detection.
  • Count often wrong: tighten the watch, calibrate the second position or recalibrate; mark by hand and send a diagnostic recording.
  • Phone and watch history differ: turn on sync on both devices and tap Sync now; use Retry sync.
  • Alarm did not ring: check watch volume and silent mode; opening the watch app renews the nearest alarm. To check the phone alarm, use Test the alarm in Settings (section 3).

More answers: support page (FAQ). Contact: salahsensetracking@gmail.com — please include device model, OS version and app version. The app version and the sources are under Settings → About.

How your data is handled: Privacy Policy. No account, ads, analytics or server; your data stays on your devices.

13. Quran memorization

  • The Memorize tab on the phone helps you memorize the Quran. In New memorization, choose Surah (first and last ayah), Page (the 604-page Madinah Mushaf layout) or Juz and tap Add. An ayah already in your list is not added twice.
  • Each day, ayahs due for review come first, then new ayahs up to New per day: … ayahs. The Ayahs row opens the day's session; tapping an entry in the list opens only its ayahs.
  • Today has three rows: Ayahs, Words and Words ayah by ayah. Each row shows what is left; tap it to open that part. Continue at the bottom opens the next part; the estimated time is shown at the top.
  • The ayah is shown in Arabic and, if you like, with its transliteration. Tap the large Tap each time you recite area each time you recite it; once a new ayah reaches Each ayah: … repetitions, it counts as learned and comes back for review the next day.
  • While you recite, the highlight moves word by word; the card below shows that word in Arabic with its transliteration. With Auto advance off, use Previous word and Next word or tap a word; each tap starts the highlight again from the beginning.
  • A translation in the app language appears below the ayah; for English it is Saheeh International. Translations are taken unchanged from QuranEnc.com; the translator or publisher and the version are shown under the translation, and Footnote opens a footnote when there is one. On the repetition screen the translation starts hidden; open it with Show translation.
  • Test with hidden words hides each word except its first letter; tap a word to reveal it, or Show all. Then choose Hard · tomorrow, Good · in … days or Easy · in … days. The intervals are 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days; Hard · tomorrow starts the ayah over. Repeating an ayah that is not yet due does not change the schedule. Translation hint shows the translation; if you use the hint, you can choose at most Good · in … days.
  • Words of learned ayahs join your word deck: tap the Words row under Today. Each day, words due for review come first, then at most 10 new words; a word that occurs in several ayahs is asked once. The card shows the word highlighted inside its ayah; recall its meaning, then tap Show. The back shows the transliteration, Translation of the ayah and how many times the word occurs in the Quran in this form. Then choose Don't know (the word starts over), Hard or Know it; the intervals are the same as for ayahs. If you choose Hard · tomorrow when testing an ayah, its words also come back by the next day. The word's meaning and type in that ayah are shown in the word panel; tap the occurrence count on the back to open it.
  • Words ayah by ayah teaches the words of the ayahs in your list ayah by ayah, in surah and ayah order. Only words not yet in your deck are asked; a studied word enters the deck right away. Use the arrows in the title to go to the previous or next ayah, or tap the title to choose a surah and ayah; your place is saved. When an ayah is done, choose Next ayah or Memorize this ayah. The Words first button on a new ayah's repetition screen also opens this screen.
  • Tap a word in the ayah to open the word panel: its transliteration, its position in the ayah, and how many times this form occurs in the Quran and in your list. Where it occurs lists every place the form occurs (All or In my list); each row shows the word's position and transliteration in that ayah; tap a row to open the ayah with the word marked, together with its translation. Tapping the occurrence count on the back of a Words card also opens the panel. The panel and the list also show the word's meaning and type in that ayah: the meaning comes from Quran.com (needs internet; where no translation exists in your language, the English meaning is shown with an EN tag) and the type from the Quranic Arabic Corpus; the sources are shown at the bottom of the list. For words with a root, the panel also shows the root and a Where the root occurs button. In the list, Same word shows only this form and Root shows every form of the root (for يَوْمِ, for example, يَوْمَ, ٱلْيَوْمَ, أَيَّامٍ… from the root ي و م); use the form chips at the top to narrow it to one form. The root also comes from the Quranic Arabic Corpus; words without a root, such as particles and pronouns, don't show this choice.
  • Memorization settings: Reminder and Time (21:00 by default), new ayahs per day and repetitions, Show transliteration by default (in Memorization review, Words ayah by ayah, Test with hidden words and the ayah view, the Show transliteration / Hide transliteration link changes it for the session) and Transliteration system: DIN 31635 (international, default) or İSAM / TDV. The transliteration shows the sounds of the letters; learn tajweed from a teacher.
  • Reading comfort (in Memorization settings): Arabic text size (default Large), Page tone (Automatic, White, Cream, Night; Automatic uses cream in light mode and night in dark mode), Highlight color (Blue by default, Green, Orange, Purple, Burgundy; the highlighted word in the ayah, in the transliteration and in excerpts uses this color), Meaning color (Dark by default), Word type color (Soft by default), Show translation by default and Haptic feedback. Highlights on memorization screens are thin underlines.
  • The reminder comes only on days with reviews or new ayahs. Memorization is on the phone only; the watch has no memorization section. Your memorization list is stored only on this phone.
  • The Quran text is the Uthmani text of the Tanzil Project (tanzil.net), used unchanged (CC BY 3.0); the transliteration is generated from it.
  • Qibla is no longer a separate tab; open it with the button at the top of the Prayer times screen.
