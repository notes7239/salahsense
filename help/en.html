Salah Sense Help

Using the app and its settings · phone and watch

Important: Automatic rakah and sajdah tracking requires a watch (Apple WatchWear OS watch); a phone alone cannot measure wrist movement. Detection is an aid and can make mistakes: watch position, strap fit and personal movement differences affect it. You can always correct records by hand. The app does not issue religious rulings or judge whether a prayer is valid.

For FAQs and contact details see the support page. Bold words are button and menu names in the app.

1. Getting started

  • The iPhoneAndroid phone app shows prayer times, qibla, dhikr, history and settings. The Apple WatchWear OS watch app follows your movements during prayer and handles adhan alerts and calibration.
  • First open the Settings tabthe Location and settings page on the phone and set location, calculation method, Asr calculation and Gender (sections 2 and 8).
  • Then calibrate once on the watch; automatic tracking is only reliable after that.
  • Without a watch you can still use prayer times, qibla, dhikr, manual Mark prayed records, make-up prayers, statistics, the Hijri calendar, the imsakiyah and widgets.
  • Prayer Guide has offline summaries, with sources, on the daily prayers and sajdah sahw.
  • To share history, turn on Share prayer history with my paired iPhone/Apple Watch in Settings → Paired device on the iPhone and Sync history in Device pairing on the watch. It is off by default; Sync now also sends older records.
  • Phone–watch sync needs the app on both devices; without a connection, records stay on the device. If needed, use Location and settings → Retry sync.
  • Change the language in Settings on the phone and Language on the watch; each device keeps its own choice.

2. Location and prayer times

  • iPhone: Settings → Location and time zone. Tap Use my location and allow location, or fill in City, Latitude, Longitude and IANA time zone (e.g. Europe/London), then tap Save city/location.
  • Location and settings: tap Get my location, or fill in Location name, Latitude, Longitude and Time zone, then tap Save.
  • Location permission is optional; you can enter the city and coordinates by hand. Update location and time zone when you travel.
  • Calculation method (12 methods): Türkiye (Diyanet), Muslim World League, Egyptian General Authority, Karachi (Univ. of Islamic Sciences), Umm al-Qura (Makkah), Dubai, Moonsighting Committee, North America (ISNA), Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Tehran. Choose the one closest to your local timetable.
  • Asr calculation: Standard uses a shadow of one object length (first Asr), Hanafi two lengths (second Asr). The Turkish timetable uses standard.
  • Madhab: Sunni (including sunnah and witr) or Shafi'i (fard). It only sets which rakahs are tracked, not the times. On the watch: Madhab / rakahs.
  • Times refresh after a change; tap Refresh prayer times if needed. On the watch, method and Asr are under Calculation method.
  • Times are calculated on the device. The app is not an official timetable and may differ from yours by a few minutes.

3. Notifications and alarms

Prayer time notification

  • Notify when prayer time begins sends a notification for each prayer time. Set the timing with the stepper: At prayer time, or up to 60 minutes before (… min before) or after (… min after). The watch has the same setting in Adhan alerts.
  • When the watch handles these notifications, the iPhone does not schedule its own; they reach the watch while the iPhone is locked.
  • Location and settings: turn on Reminder and choose At prayer time or … min before. If notifications are late, grant Scheduling permission.

Alarm before the time ends

  • Alarm before the time ends: Off or a number of minutes before the prayer time ends. If the prayer is not recorded as prayed, it rings even in silent mode.
  • It may still ring if a record detected by the watch has not reached the phone yet.
  • A real alarm needs iOS 26; otherwise, or without alarm permission, a notification is sent instead.
  • 45 and 30 minutes before the end, the watch plays the hayya part of the adhan with vibration (Come to prayer, come to success), and once more strongly if you are asleep. If you seem to be sitting or lying idle while there is time, you get one You seem available notification (never in a prohibited time).

Tahajjud and Fajr alerts (watch)

  • In Adhan alertsAdhan alerts on the watch, Tahajjud alert follows the last third of the night and Fajr adhan alert follows imsak.
  • Alert time: At prayer time or 5–60 minutes before. Snooze: 1–15 minutes.
  • Moving silences the alarm briefly; tapping Mark prayed stops it. Silencing movement sets how much movement is needed: Less movement (sensitive), Medium, More movement (hard).
  • The nearest alarm plays your adhan sound, other alerts use the notification sound. Opening the watch app renews the nearest alarm.
  • Sound and vibration come from the device notification channel (Alert sound and vibration); the app has no built-in adhan recording.

Adhan sound (Apple Watch)

  • Adhan sound (or Adhan alerts → Choose adhan sound): enter the https address of a CAF, AIFF or WAV file and tap Download. Up to 30 seconds plays in notifications; longer files play only in the app.
  • Select an installed sound; use Play, Stop and Delete. With none selected, the built-in alarm sound plays.
  • Hayya segment (repeat + vibration): mark the "Hayya ala's-salah, Hayya ala'l-falah" part with Start and End seconds, set Repeat, and test with Play segment.
  • The file is downloaded directly from that address; the server is not ours.

Notification permission, silent mode, Focus, battery saving and volume can all affect alerts.

4. Calibration on the watch

Calibration teaches the watch your own takbir, qiyam, ruku, sajdah and sitting postures. It is done on the watch only.

  1. Wear the watch snugly on your usual wrist (right or left).
  2. Calibration → Personal calibration wizard → Start spoken calibrationCalibration → Start spoken calibration.
  3. Follow the voice: takbir (hands raised), qiyam, ruku, standing up, sajdah, sitting between sajdahs, second sajdah, final sitting, salam right, salam left. After each instruction you have time to take the posture, then a short measurement follows; hold still comfortably.
  4. With Learn the prayer-ending gesture (optional) on, wiping the hands over the face after salam is also measured; or tap Skip the prayer gesture.
  5. Stop pauses; Resume this step continues.
  6. At the end you see Calibration complete and your wrist heights relative to qiyam. Tap Done, return to prayer.
  • Salam is voice-guided only; head movement is not measured.
  • After a successful calibration, continuous detection turns on automatically.
  • If postures were too similar or the takbir was not recognised, restart on the same wrist and hold your hands raised in the takbir step.

Second position

  • If the watch slides down your wrist during prayer, teach that position too: after the normal calibration tap Calibrate second position, slide the watch 1–2 cm towards the hand and repeat the steps (including wiping the face).
  • Detection uses whichever position fits better. A new normal calibration keeps the second position; if the strap fit changed, use Recalibrate second position or Delete second position.

When to recalibrate

  • When the watch position, strap fit or wrist changes, or detection is often wrong. A Check the watch position notification also means the watch is loose.
  • The app also learns wrist heights from your prayers. After a big change use Reset learned heights; Delete calibration removes the whole calibration.
  • Voice practice lets you practise with voice guidance; it creates no record.

5. Automatic tracking during prayer

  • Open the watch app within the prayer time and start with the opening takbir. Each part (sunnah, fard, witr) starts with its own takbir. When raised hands are seen, Takbir posture detected; move into qiyam. appears.
  • The order follows the opening takbirs: Fajr 2+2 · Dhuhr 4+4+2 · Asr 4+4 · Maghrib 3+2 · Isha 4+4+2+3 witr. Maghrib starts with the fard. With Shafi'i (fard), only the fard is tracked.
  • The screen shows the current part, the next takbir and the rakahs detected.
  • To start directly with the fard, tap Skip the first sunnahStart with fard.
  • Sajdah sahw is made at the end of the prayer, in the final sitting; if detected, the record shows Sahw prostration performed.
  • Before sajdah sahw you can tap I will perform prostrations of forgetfulness; the app then expects two extra sajdahs and the final sitting. I will repeat this section restarts the current part. If saving failed, tap Retry saving.
  • If calibration is missing, a Personal calibration wizard button appears.

Wrong count or missed prayer

  • If too many or too few rakahs are detected, the watch warns you (Check the rakat count). Check your count.
  • Mark the prayer by hand: Mark fard prayedMark prayed on the watch, Mark prayed on the phone.
  • If you prayed on time but detection missed it, after the time ends open History on the phone, tap Mark prayed on time, set Time you prayed and tap Save as prayed. The time must be within the prayer time; this cannot be undone.
  • You can undo a wrong record in history with Undo record and then tap Mark prayed again.

Continuous detection and battery

  • Detection settingsPrayer detection → Continuous detection (experimental) keeps detection running with the screen off or on the watch face.
  • For this the watch keeps a workout session open all day. No workout is saved to Health, but battery use rises and Activity rings may be affected. If the session stops, reopen the app.
  • Background detection drains the battery faster. If the app is force-stopped, reopen it.
  • When it is off, detection works only while the app is open.

Optional prayers

  • Tahajjud and Duha times appear on the prayer times screen; mark them within their time with Mark prayed · +500. The watch can also detect them from the opening takbir.
  • Fajr make-up → Duha: choose a recorded Fajr make-up prayer, then the Duha rakahs are detected in order.

6. Sequence check and confirmation

Before a detected fard is saved, the sequence is checked: opening takbir, a ruku and two sajdahs in each rakah, the final sitting, sajdahs in the same direction, and no walking-like turn.

  • If the check fails, the watch vibrates and asks Did you pray the … fard? with the reasons. The time list shows Awaiting confirmation.
  • Yes, I prayed: saved with the detected finish time as Automatic detection (confirmed).
  • No: nothing is saved; reminders for that prayer time return.
  • No answer by the end of the prayer time: saved as Automatic detection (not confirmed, please check), so a performed prayer is not lost. Tapping Mark prayed meanwhile removes the question.

Marked by: … labels

  • Automatic detection: the fard passed the check.
  • Automatic detection (confirmed) / Automatic detection (not confirmed, please check): see above.
  • Automatic detection (fallback record, please check): detection counted the fard's rakahs exactly, but the normal save step failed, so a backup path wrote it.
  • “Prayed” on watch, “Prayed” in watch notification, “Prayed” on phone, “Prayed” in phone notification, “Prayed on time” correction, Mark made up: manual marks.
  • Labels asking you to check are orange in history; review and correct if needed.

7. History, make-up prayers and score

  • The History tabThe History page on the phone shows each prayer as Prayed, Made up or Pending / missed, with Sahw prostration performed and the marked-by label where relevant.
  • Prayer times that end unrecorded go to the make-up list (Make-up prayers due: …). When you make one up, tap Mark prayed next to that prayer; on the watch use Make-up prayers; after a time ends its button becomes Mark made up.
  • Score: praying earlier in the time scores higher; a missed prayer lowers the score. Optional prayers add a bonus (Mark prayed · +500).
  • Statistics: weekly, monthly and yearly summaries and punctuality.
  • Create backup file saves your fard history to a file you choose; Restore backup merges it back.
  • A missing record does not prove a prayer was missed; the app only keeps marks made by you and by detection.

8. Excused days

  • This section appears only when SettingsLocation and settings → Gender is Female. On the iPhone it is under Calculation and reminders; the watch menu has a Gender row. With Select or Male it is hidden; changing the choice does not delete saved periods.
  • Settings → Excused days (also in the watch menu)Excused days: tap Start period when a period begins and End period when it ends. A warning appears if a period stays open long.
  • Edit past periods with Edit (Start, End, Ongoing) and Save, or remove them with Delete period.
  • During a period there are no prayer time, suhoor or iftar notifications, no make-up debt or score; detection and Mark prayed are off and Tahajjud/Duha tracking pauses. Times and widgets show Excused day.
  • Only you enter periods. They are stored on your device and, with sync on, shared with your other device along with history.

9. Hijri calendar and Ramadan

  • The Hijri date on the times screen follows the Umm al-Qura calendar. If it is a day off from your local calendar, choose −1, 0 or +1 in Settings → Hijri calendarLocation and settings → Hijri date adjustment.
  • Religious days lists upcoming religious days; the next one also appears on the times screen.
  • Ramadan timetable shows imsak and iftar times for Ramadan.
  • Suhoor reminder: Off or … min before imsak (30, 45, 60, 90). Iftar notification notifies at iftar. Both are scheduled only in Ramadan.
  • Fasting: in Ramadan the times screen shows the countdown to imsak or iftar and an I fasted button; a marked day shows Fasted.

10. Widgets, Live Activity and complications

  • The Next prayer widget counts down to the next prayer time or the end of the current one. Add it to the Home Screen (small, medium) or Lock Screen: touch and hold the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Salah Sense.
  • Live Activity (in Settings) shows a countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island; it does not start during an excused period.
  • Apple Watch complications: edit the watch face and choose Salah Sense (rectangular, inline, circular, corner).
  • The Android Home Screen widget shows the next prayer time; touch and hold the Home Screen and add Salah Sense from the widgets.
  • Wear OS offers a Salah Sense tile and complication.

During an excused period, widgets and complications show Excused day.

11. Sending a diagnostic recording

If detection keeps getting a prayer wrong, a diagnostic recording helps us investigate.

  1. On the watch: Detection settings → Diagnostic recording → Start recording. Wait 3 seconds with your hands down, pray all parts, then tap End recording. A recording lasts at most 20 minutes.
  2. On the watch, turn on Diagnostic recording on the Prayer detection page while detection runs, then pray.
  3. Send recordings to iPhone (with the app open on the iPhone); if needed Resend latest recordingSend recordings to phone.
  4. On the phone: Settings → Diagnostic recordings → choose the recording → Share recordingPrayer detection → Share, and email it to salahsensetracking@gmail.com.
  • Recording with the screen off needs continuous detection.
  • For each fard saved by detection, a short Automatic fard summary of the last minutes is kept and can be shared the same way.
  • Recordings contain motion sensor values, pressure/relative altitude, detection state, calibration data and app version; no location, audio, name or account data. They leave the device only if you share them. Recent recordings are kept; delete with Delete recording.

12. Troubleshooting and support

  • Times differ: check calculation method, Asr calculation, location and time zone, then tap Refresh prayer times.
  • No notifications: check notification permission, silent mode and Focus in the device settings. Battery saving and Scheduling permission matter too.
  • Detection does not start: on No motion sensor data received. reopen the watch app; on Takbir measurement is missing. Start spoken calibration. calibrate; for screen-off use turn on continuous detection.
  • Count often wrong: tighten the watch, calibrate the second position or recalibrate; mark by hand and send a diagnostic recording.
  • Phone and watch history differ: turn on sync on both devices and tap Sync now; use Retry sync.
  • Alarm did not ring: check watch volume and silent mode; opening the watch app renews the nearest alarm.
